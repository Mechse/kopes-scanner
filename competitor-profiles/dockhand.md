# Dockhand — quick competitor profile

Researched: 2026-11-08. Category: Direct: Docker operators.

## Observed product and pricing

Docker and Compose management with Grype/Trivy scanning, CVE reporting and safe-pull protection during updates. Free edition includes vulnerability scanning; paid tiers add commercial licensing/support and enterprise controls.

BSL 1.1 source-available. Free edition; commercial SMB $499 per Dockhand host/year; Enterprise $1,499 per Dockhand host/year. The vendor defines host as the machine running Dockhand.

Sources: [Source 1](https://dockhand.pro/).

## Interpretation for Kopes

High relevance: competes for the same operator and bundles scanning into operations. Do not call it OSI open source. Kopes should demonstrate useful host-package coverage and an easy side-by-side Coolify installation.

## Limits

Documentation research only; no installation or performance comparison. Traffic, market share, funding, customer counts and review scores were not assessed. No SEO data provider was available for this research. Missing documented features are not confirmed absences.
