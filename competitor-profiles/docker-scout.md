# Docker Scout — quick competitor profile

Researched: 2026-11-08. Category: Adjacent: image and software supply-chain security.

## Observed product and pricing

Image component inventory/SBOM matched against a continuously updated vulnerability database; CLI, Docker Hub and hosted dashboard integrations.

Commercial service with free Personal allowance of 1 Scout-enabled repository. Docker Pro: $11/user/month monthly or $9 annual equivalent, 2 repositories. Team: $16 monthly or $15 annual equivalent, unlimited Scout repositories.

Sources: [Source 1](https://docs.docker.com/scout/), [Source 2](https://www.docker.com/pricing/).

## Interpretation for Kopes

Strong substitute for image analysis, especially for existing Docker customers. Its documented workflow is image/repository-oriented rather than Kopes' combined single-host inventory. Docker subscription prices are not a per-server scanner quote.

## Limits

Documentation research only; no installation or performance comparison. Traffic, market share, funding, customer counts and review scores were not assessed. No SEO data provider was available for this research. Missing documented features are not confirmed absences.
