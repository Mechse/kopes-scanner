# Snyk Container — quick competitor profile

Researched: 2026-11-08. Category: Adjacent: developer security.

## Observed product and pricing

Container and dependency scans, base-image upgrade recommendations, CI integrations, continuous monitoring and Kubernetes workload context.

Commercial platform with free tier. Current pricing lists Team starting at $25/month; this is a platform starting price, not a verified all-inclusive Container deployment quote. Enterprise pricing is custom.

Sources: [Source 1](https://snyk.io/product/container-vulnerability-management/), [Source 2](https://snyk.io/plans/).

## Interpretation for Kopes

Useful benchmark for actionable remediation. Broader developer workflow than Kopes' single-server install; verify Container bundle, limits and deployment requirements before pricing comparisons.

## Limits

Documentation research only; no installation or performance comparison. Traffic, market share, funding, customer counts and review scores were not assessed. No SEO data provider was available for this research. Missing documented features are not confirmed absences.
