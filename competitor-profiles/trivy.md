# Trivy — quick competitor profile

Researched: 2026-11-08. Category: DIY substitute and underlying engine.

## Observed product and pricing

Scanner supports container images, filesystems, repositories, VM images and Kubernetes; vulnerability, misconfiguration, secret and SBOM capabilities.

Free, Apache-2.0 open source.

Sources: [Source 1](https://github.com/aquasecurity/trivy).

## Interpretation for Kopes

Kopes already uses Trivy. Users can script the engine themselves; differentiation must come from discovery, context, history and an easier response workflow. No independent detection-accuracy advantage is established.

## Limits

Documentation research only; no installation or performance comparison. Traffic, market share, funding, customer counts and review scores were not assessed. No SEO data provider was available for this research. Missing documented features are not confirmed absences.
