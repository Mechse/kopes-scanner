L27: Why Dependency-Track
L28: ## One platform for your entire software supply chain
L29: 
L30: Built around the software bill of materials, it tracks every component in every version of every project, and surfaces risk the moment it emerges.
L31: 
L32: ### Inventory
L33: 
L34: Know what you ship.
L35: 
L36: Ingest CycloneDX SBOMs and track libraries, containers, operating systems, firmware, and services across every version of every project in your portfolio.

L38: 
L39: See risk as it emerges.
L40: 
L41: Continuously match every component against multiple vulnerability sources, verify upstream integrity, and prioritize what matters with EPSS.
L42: 
L43: ### Govern
L44: 
L45: Enforce your standards.
L46:

L98: New in 5.0
L99: ## The largest redesign in the project's history
L100: 
L101: Codenamed Hyades, v5 rebuilds how Dependency-Track scales, survives failure, and reasons about risk: horizontal scaling and active/active high availability, durable processing that resumes after a crash, supply chain integrity verification, and an expression-based policy engine.
